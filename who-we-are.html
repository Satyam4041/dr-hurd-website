About Dr. Hurd

Dr. Hurd is a licensed Psychologist with over 40 years of experience providing psychological therapy, biofeedback, neurotherapy, and hypnosis to clients. As the former Director of Health, Nutrition, and Mental Health for the New York City Agency for Child Development's Head Start Program, Dr. Hurd trained over 2,000 staff members and served a student population of more than 16,000 children and their parents.

Dr. Hurd holds a Doctorate of Philosophy in Psychology from Yeshiva University. She is a Certified Biofeedback and EEG Neurotherapist, Certified Hypnotherapist, and was awarded Certification of Proficiency from the APA College of Proficiency in the treatment of Substance Abuse.

She is certified at the diplomate level in QEEG-DL analysis and holds Diplomate Status in Neurotherapy from the National Registry of Neurofeedback and QEEG Providers, as well as from the Neurotherapy Certification Board. Additionally, she has Fellow Certification in EEG Biofeedback and Peripheral Biofeedback from the Biofeedback Certification Association of America (BCIA).

Dr. Hurd is the former Chairperson of the International QEEG Certification Board and the former Editor of the International Society for Neurofeedback and QEEG Research’s newsletter, NeuroConnection. She has authored chapters in several leading neuroscience publications.

Denise Hurd
